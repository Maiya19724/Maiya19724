## Selected research

### Genome mining leads to dioxanopeptins

**Chemical Science · 2026** · Co-first author

Using lipid-donor biosynthesis as a genomic starting point, our study identified the *dxp* gene-cluster family and linked it to antibacterial lipopeptides. I contributed the biosynthetic gene-cluster analysis.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6sc00003g) · [Study details](https://maiya19724.github.io/publication/2026-02-27-dioxanopeptins/)

### An unexpected route to polyketide chain release

**JACS · 2023** · Co-first author

Our work uncovered a ketoreductase domain that releases a fungal polyketide chain, revealing an unusual route to the salicylaldehyde scaffold.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.3c02011)
